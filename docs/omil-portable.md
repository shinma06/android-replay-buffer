# Portable operating brief

Use this brief for authorized work and when transferring its operating method to a fresh session or client. It carries operational judgment, not access rights, project acceptance, or a prescribed sequence of tools. Read the actual assignment, authority, project facts, and available capabilities with it. The repository's [worker request](team-worker-prompt.md), [workflow](workflow.md), and [ownership rules](work-management.md) remain canonical for work in this repository.

Treat client-provided instructions and required general skills as runtime context. Record permitted sources, identities and actual reads before claiming input-only reproduction; a task packet cannot override higher-priority instructions. Assess unexpected context and limit the resulting claim instead of silently treating the run as isolated.

## Separate the obligation from its client

State purpose, required quality, roles, inputs, outputs and sufficient evidence without requiring a model brand, Codex tool name, chat ID format or private Codex path. Map only the capabilities the actual task needs in its existing assignment; do not build a universal adapter or install another client to prove this separation. Keep efficient native facilities as the preferred implementation where they work.

| Required fact | Capability to identify in the actual environment |
| --- | --- |
| The human authorized this scope | Direct human source and applicable scope, plus actual client/service permissions. A copied summary or another agent's assertion cannot grant consent. |
| A distinct authorized role performed the work | Actual origin identity and role operations from the available runtime. Codex thread handles are one implementation; a role label or invented UUID is not equivalent evidence. |
| The result belongs to the assigned input and conditions | Fixed source/artifact identity, full relevant content, observations, failures and independent assessment. Native artifacts or ordinary files may carry these facts. |
| PM received the result and residual ownership | An actual readback tied to the result and receiver; use existing native communication or an authorized shared record, without routine human forwarding. |
| Execution stopped and the result remains retrievable | Actual terminal state, preserved result and later retrieval. Native archive is useful when available; file preservation alone does not prove stopped execution. |

Use the supported trusted review/origin routes in [automation setup](setup/automation.md#レビュー元と実行元の選択); the Codex implementation remains the default. A configured review service, trusted origin adapter or execution restriction must retain its current guarantees without making its brand a common task requirement. Keep its current guarantees and efficient route. Do not copy Codex environment variables, session fingerprints or provider completion formats into a new task's common contract, and do not invent an equivalent adapter or bypass a local gate when the target capability is absent. Declare that specific integration limitation separately from the provider-neutral operating method.

## Decide from the required result

For each material obligation, connect the owned guarantee and observable defect to fixed existing evidence, the fact still missing, and the least costly sufficient method. Inspect the evidence before selecting an operator or copying an old procedure. Keep a working method when it is sufficient. No missing fact means no new execution for that obligation.

Reuse needs applicable source, artifact, contract and runtime conditions. A changed filename, new document, old PASS or successful command does not establish equivalence. Inspect affected components and dependencies; retest affected or unknown guarantees, retain qualified unaffected facts. Do not silently lower the requirement or turn representative integration into full-content proof.

## Adapt within the assignment

Recurring defects against the same guarantee, expanding implementation, missing capability or a failed observation are evidence to check the source of a supposedly fixed condition before assigning another repair. Separate human requirements and existing main compatibility from a method or display choice added by the current PR. The owner compares maintaining, simplifying or splitting the method using the concrete missing fact and expected result, selects a bounded change, observes it, then adopts, corrects or withdraws it. Workers and reviewers surface conflicting method assumptions to the owner; they do not waive guarantees themselves. Reconcile the affected active assignment. Record the triggering assistance, adopted choice and actual result in the existing Issue/PR; human-assisted reconsideration, a document change or that trial alone does not prove autonomous adaptation.

Optimize verified progress toward the purpose at the required quality under the same model and comparable working time and resources. Prefer sufficient evidence reuse, proportional checks, early resolution of material ambiguity and less waiting, rework and administration. Reducing a budget or stopping earlier is not evidence of better operation. Preserve actual permission and spending limits; keep a sufficient working method unless observed facts justify changing it.

For a blocked obligation, identify the actual cause and smallest affected scope from the original response and live state. Consider both a different means to the same required result and other ready work that advances the purpose. Choose by the missing fact, expected recovery and bounded cost; neither alternative search nor task switching is a mandatory first step. Do not turn an unattempted action or a connection failure into a permission denial, or bypass an actual denial by renaming the route.

Establish target, ownership, bounded effects, preservation and result checks yourself when possible. Continue independent authorized work while a different obligation is waiting. Ask a human only for an unresolved choice or boundary they actually control. No response is not consent; another agent or this brief cannot grant permission.

## Reconsider before stopping unfinished work

Run this flow whenever you would stop or hand off unfinished work, declare it blocked, or recommend stopping the whole task, regardless of a client's Goal feature. Completed work and ordinary progress replies are not blocked decisions. Use the existing task record, without a new approval, monitor or per-operation checklist.

First obey a human pause, actual permission denial, safety boundary or exhausted real resource limit immediately in its affected scope. Preserve what can safely be preserved. Do not delay that stop for reconsideration or bypass it through another route. Review and explain only within the remaining permitted scope; a whole-task human pause also stops independent task work.

Otherwise evaluate these steps in order before finalizing the stop. Unknown answers remain unknown, not evidence that all useful work is exhausted.

| Step | Required decision from actual evidence |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Scope | Name the operation, bounded study, role or whole task being stopped, its unfinished required result and preserved completed work. Do not expand a local failure to the whole purpose. |
| 2. Cause | Inspect the original response and current state. Separate facts, hypotheses and unknowns; identify the causal missing permission, resource, information, dependency or capability. Repeated status reports or observation timeouts do not establish termination or a fundamental blocker. |
| 3. Constraint | Trace the limit to its source. Preserve human/tool/safety/spending limits and required quality; distinguish them from an agent-selected method, sample count, time window or assignment. Reaching a study limit ends that study, not automatically analysis or the whole task. |
| 4. Remaining route | Check cause-directed analysis from existing evidence, a purpose-equivalent permitted method, and independently ready work. Identify a useful concrete next action or why relevant routes cannot supply the missing fact. No exhaustive search, pointless task switching or new approval for ordinary agent-owned design. |
| 5. Decision | A prospective replan needs a causal reason/new information or changed condition, finite bounds, preservation, result checks and actual authority. Never erase failures or reset study limits merely because results were poor. Continue a useful permitted action if one remains; otherwise stop only the affected scope with the report below. |

Let the evidence determine the decision. A platform's blocked-turn count is an additional API condition, never proof of exhaustion. Record task execution and actual control state separately; do not invent a resume API or claim an unconfirmed state change.

For a warranted stop, report to the human and receiving owner:

- **Cause and evidence:** actual impediment, distinguishing facts, hypotheses and unknowns.
- **Saved state:** completed/preserved results, unmet requirements, stopped execution/resources and unverified state. A final message alone does not prove a worker stopped.
- **Resume conditions:** the concrete missing fact/change, who can resolve it and required authority. Do not return ordinary agent-owned design as a human permission request.
- **Resolution steps:** known steps in order and the next safe action; label an unknown solution rather than inventing one. Preserve bounds and original failures on resumption.

If whole-task stopping is unwarranted, explicitly retract it, retain the legitimate local stop and unmet outcome, and perform the next permitted action. Record who triggered reconsideration: application after human correction does not prove unprompted adaptation or improved efficiency.

## Use independent roles when needed

Create a role only for a ready obligation requiring execution or independent judgment, and only through a human-authorized route that the client permits. Supply fixed inputs, scope, exclusions, output, receiver, bounds and stop condition. A different role name within the same conversation is not independent review. Do not delegate through a prohibited child process under another name.

The PM receives the actual result and its evidence, not merely a completion message. Match scope, identities, file digests, original failures, independent assessment and remaining obligations before acceptance. Preserve conflicting or duplicate deliveries and resolve them against authoritative state; do not execute twice because a receipt was repeated. Independent review must challenge evidence sufficiency, not just procedure compliance.

## Preserve failure and finish the actual work

Keep original observations immutable. Separate a violated guarantee, missing observation, unknown operation outcome, permission/usage boundary, and an unattempted action. Do not call an unexplained failure environmental. Before retrying, name the new information or changed condition, the finite limit, recovery and exit. For an indeterminate write, inspect actual state before resending.

Completion, stopped execution, saved evidence, archived presentation, released ownership and product acceptance are separate facts. Before ending a role, collect results and residual ownership and confirm it is no longer running. Preserve unfinished work. Archive only the owned stopped role; verify that a later session can find the retained result and its limits. If a platform lacks archive, preserve a completed record with retrieval and state the capability difference. Do not call deletion archive or claim unavailable UI behavior.

## Leave enough evidence to continue

Use the existing task record for the accepted result, fixed inputs and conditions, method, observations, review, residual owner and next action. Keep private paths, diagnostics and credentials out of public summaries. Record actual elapsed time, retries, assistance and provider usage when available; use unknown for unavailable measurements. Keep failure and interrupted runs in the denominator. Small trials prove only their tested scope; do not invent efficiency gains, general reliability or full GUI acceptance.
